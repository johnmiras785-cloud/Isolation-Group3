What is isolation?

Isolation happens when a population is separated into groups that can no longer breed with each other.

🚧Separation
🔀No gene flow
🧬Groups change apart

Natural barriers that separate populations

🏔️MountainsA mountain range keeps groups on opposite sides from meeting.
🌊RiversA new river can split one forest population into two.
🏝️OceansIslands trap animals far from the mainland, like Galápagos finches.
🏜️DesertsDry land blocks forest species from crossing over.
